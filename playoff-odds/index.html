How the odds are worked out
  • Records, points for, points against and max points come from the Sleeper rosters endpoint after week 9. The schedule comes from the Sleeper matchups endpoint for weeks 10–15.
  • Each team gets a strength rating: 70% their actual points per game, 30% their max-possible points per game (scaled to how efficiently the league sets lineups), then pulled 25% toward the league average so nine games don't over-decide it.
  • Every remaining week, each team's score is drawn from a normal distribution around its rating with a spread of 22 points. The season was replayed 100,000 times.
  • Top 4 and bottom 4 are the share of those seasons a team finished 1st–4th or 7th–10th. The odds don't account for injuries, byes, trades or waiver moves.
  • This is a snapshot, not live: it won't change until it's re-run with new Sleeper data.
