How the odds are worked out
  • Every score, result and fixture comes from the Sleeper matchups endpoint for weeks 1–15. Every week's scores match Sleeper's season totals and records exactly.
  • Each team gets a strength rating from its points per game, pulled toward the league average. After n games a team's own average counts for n/(n+10) of the rating, so early weeks lean on the league average and later weeks on the team. The 10 comes from this season's data: weekly scores swing by about 25 points, much more than the real gaps between teams.
  • Every remaining week, each team's score is drawn from a normal distribution around its rating with a spread of 25 points. The season is replayed 50,000 times.
  • The trend charts repeat this after every week from 1 to 11, using only the results known at that point.
  • Top 4 and bottom 4 are the share of those seasons a team finished 1st–4th or 7th–10th. The odds don't account for injuries, byes, trades or waiver moves.
  • This is a snapshot, not live: it won't change until it's re-run with new Sleeper data.
